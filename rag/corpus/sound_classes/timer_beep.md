# Timer Beep

## Description
A timer beep is a short, repetitive electronic tone produced by kitchen timers, microwave ovens, washing machines, dishwashers, and other appliances to signal that a cycle or countdown has completed.

## Typical Scenarios
- Kitchen timer or microwave oven signalling that cooking is done
- Washing machine or dishwasher end-of-cycle alert
- Oven pre-heat completion tone
- Medication reminder alarm on a phone or smart device
- Smoke alarm low-battery chirp (may be confused)

## Safety Implications
**LOW.** Timer beeps are routine informational alerts.
- Ignoring a cooking timer for an extended period could lead to overcooked or burnt food and, in extreme cases, a fire risk.
- For users with medication schedules, a missed timer alert may be significant — SoundSight can help ensure these are not missed.

## Common False Positives
- Other electronic devices beeping (e.g., uninterruptible power supplies, smart home hubs)
- Low-battery chirp from a smoke detector (similar single-beep pattern, but typically less frequent)
- Notification sounds from mobile phones or tablets
- Video game sound effects
