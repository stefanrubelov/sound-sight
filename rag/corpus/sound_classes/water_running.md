# Water Running

## Description
The continuous sound of flowing or running water — from taps, showers, pipes, or appliances. Duration varies from a few seconds (hand-washing) to many minutes (shower or bath filling).

## Typical Scenarios
- Shower or bath in use
- Kitchen or bathroom tap running for hand-washing or dish rinsing
- Washing machine filling cycle
- Toilet cistern refilling after flushing
- Garden hose or outdoor tap in use

## Safety Implications
**LOW to MEDIUM.** Water running for an unusually long time may indicate a forgotten tap, a leak, or an appliance malfunction.
- Water running for > 30 minutes in an unexpected context (e.g., middle of the night) may indicate a burst pipe or a tap left open accidentally.
- Leaks detected early can prevent significant water damage.
- For elderly residents living alone, an unusually long shower or running tap may warrant a welfare check.

## Common False Positives
- Rain or wind creating a flowing water sound near a window or vent
- Aquarium or water feature in the home
- HVAC or heating system producing a gurgling or flowing sound in pipes
- Audio or TV content containing water sounds
