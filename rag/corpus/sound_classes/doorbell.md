# Doorbell

## Description
A doorbell produces a short, melodic chime or buzz when a visitor presses the button at the front door. Most doorbells ring for 1–3 seconds.

## Typical Scenarios
- A visitor, delivery driver, or guest at the front door
- A neighbour ringing by mistake
- Children playing with the button
- A parcel delivery that may require a signature

## Safety Implications
**LOW.** A doorbell is a routine household event.
- If the doorbell rings unexpectedly at night, verify the visitor via a peephole or video doorbell before opening.
- Repeated rapid ringing may indicate urgency — check on the visitor.
- SoundSight users may wish to pair doorbell alerts with a flashing light or vibrating device placed near their seating area.

## Common False Positives
- Neighbouring doorbells with a similar tone
- TV or music playing a doorbell sound effect
- Notification sounds from smart devices that resemble a chime
- Testing of smart doorbell hardware
