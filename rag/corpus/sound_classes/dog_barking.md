# Dog Barking

## Description
A dog's bark is a short, rhythmic vocalisation that dogs use to communicate alertness, excitement, fear, or a territorial warning. Single barks or rapid sequences are both common.

## Typical Scenarios
- A visitor or stranger approaching the property (territorial alert)
- The dog reacting to outdoor noise, another animal, or a passing vehicle
- Excitement during play or before feeding
- Separation anxiety when left alone
- Alarm barking at night in response to an unusual sound or intruder

## Safety Implications
**LOW to MEDIUM.** Dog barking is usually routine, but sustained or frantic barking at unusual hours can be a useful early warning signal.
- Persistent barking at night, especially if unusual for the dog's normal pattern, may be worth investigating.
- If the household does not own a dog, the sound may originate from a neighbour's pet or a stray.
- SoundSight can help deaf users stay aware of their dog's communications.

## Common False Positives
- Barking heard through walls or open windows from a neighbouring dog
- TV or audio containing dog sounds
- Certain squeaky toys or doorbells with a similar acoustic profile
- Fox or other wildlife vocalisations that resemble a bark
